1. The service

VetoReview is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira and Confluence. It provides a safety checkpoint for planned actions by AI agents, automations, and humans: it risk-scores a planned action and the text that requested it, and returns a verdict of ALLOW, HOLD-FOR-REVIEW, or VETO with reasons. For held actions it can post a human approval card as a Jira comment. VetoReview never executes the reviewed action itself; final decisions always remain with the site's users and administrators.
